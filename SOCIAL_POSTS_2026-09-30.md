## LinkedIn

Campus English can look seamless. Kitchen Dutch often decides whether a mixed student house becomes a household.

Nuffic reports about 130,000 international degree students in the Netherlands in 2025-26 - roughly 17% of the student population. Many are satisfied with their studies. Far fewer are satisfied with their social life. ResearchNed’s survey for ISO, LSVb, and ESN found that 60% of international respondents had difficulty interacting with Dutch students.

That gap does not only show up in lecture halls. It shows up when the group chat switches language mid-decision, when house jokes need a footnote, and when one person becomes the unpaid interpreter for every gemeente letter.

Language is part of living infrastructure. Mixed houses that name a default language for binding decisions - and keep short bilingual recaps - treat belonging as something you schedule, not something you hope for.

What language norms has your student house actually written down?
