# Social posts - 2026-08-12

## LinkedIn

**Graduate holdover is the student housing story hiding in plain sight.**

Kences data, reported by NOS in September 2025, shows that **57% of graduates still lived in their student room one year after finishing**. Not because they want the campus lifestyle, but because the general rental market will not absorb them.

At the same time, the gap between students who want a room and students who have one has widened: 44% live out vs. 49% who want to, compared with 52% and 59% eight years earlier.

CBS figures from 2026 add another layer: only 96,000 people under 25 entered the housing market after leaving home in 2024, down from 125,000 in 2023.

New construction matters. But student rooms behave like a conveyor belt. If the exit is blocked, the queue behind it does not move, no matter how many policy papers label housing a "student experience" issue.

## Instagram

You finished your degree. Your student contract is ending. And you are still in the same room.

You are not alone. National monitoring data suggests **more than half of graduates** are still in student housing a year after graduating, not by choice, but because the next step on the rental market is out of reach.

Meanwhile, fewer students than before actually manage to move out during their studies. The gap between "I want a room" and "I have a room" keeps shrinking for the wrong reasons.

**Question for the comments:** Did you (or someone you know) stay in student housing after graduating? How long did it take to find the next place?
