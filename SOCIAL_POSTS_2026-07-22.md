## LinkedIn

Nearly half of Dutch students who graduated in 2023 never moved out during their entire degree.

New CBS and NIDI research, reported by NOS, shows 43% of that cohort lived at their parents' home for the full study period — up from 31% among 2016 graduates. The delay shows up early: 79% were still at home after year one, compared with 63% in the earlier cohort.

The pattern is strongest at HBO colleges (55% thuiswonend in 2023 vs 41% in 2016) but universities see the same direction (32% vs 19%).

Student loans are part of the story. Room shortages are another. NOS cites roughly 23,000 missing student rooms nationally, while rental reform has pulled private listings off the market.

Thuiswonen is not always a problem. It becomes one when students who want independence cannot access it — and when long commutes replace campus life.

Still living with your parents at 22? In the Netherlands, you are increasingly not alone.

New CBS figures show 43% of students who finished their degree in 2023 never moved out during study. For many, it is not a choice — it is what happens when rooms disappear and rents climb.

HBO students feel it most (55% stayed home). But the trend is everywhere.
