# Social posts - 2026-11-11

Article: [No Student Loan Interest Cap: What To Do Now](/blog/student-loan-interest-cap-scrapped-netherlands)

## 1. LinkedIn

On 3 November the Tweede Kamer voted down a motion to keep the promised 2.5% interest cap on student loans. The reason is arithmetic, not ideology: on 22 October the minister reported that the cap would now cost at least EUR 160 million a year against the EUR 40 million reserved, because the long-term rate student loans follow rose from 2.7% to 3.3%.

What it means in practice: DUO's 2027 rate is 2.70% for the repayment regime most current students are on (SF35) and 3.28% for SF15, against 2.33% and 2.29% in 2026. For 2027 the gap with the scrapped cap is small, about EUR 20 a year per EUR 10,000 of debt. The real change is that students now carry the risk of future rate moves. The remaining EUR 40 million goes mostly to tuition refunds for mbo students who leave early, plus a small bump to the planned uitwonende basisbeurs from 2028/29.

For student welfare teams and study advisers, this is a communication moment. Many students do not know their repayment regime, whether their debt is loan or prestatiebeurs, or that international EU/EEA students may qualify for DUO finance with 32 working hours a month. A short, plain-language checklist in your next intake or wellbeing newsletter could prevent a lot of quiet worry.

Sources: Rijksoverheid, ScienceGuide, AD, DUO.
