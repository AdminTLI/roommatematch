## 1. LinkedIn

Late October is when Dutch student houses stop being launchpads and start being the whole social map.

Daylight shrinks. Midterms expand. Terrace plans become kitchen plans. National student monitoring still finds about 23% of HBO and university students report strong loneliness - and living away from home can intensify that gap when the shared kitchen goes quiet.

The cultural fix is not another mandatory house party. It is **huisavond done lightly**: a fixed slot, an opt-in dinner, a lit communal room, and permission to leave early. Gezelligheid works when it is optional contact, not performance.

Institutions invest in coaches and peer groups. Retention still rises or falls at the kitchen table.
