# Social posts - 2026-09-16

## LinkedIn

Exam weeks do not invent new roommate personalities. They compress the ones already in the house.

The Monitor Mentale gezondheid en Middelengebruik Studenten 2025 (Trimbos / RIVM / GGD GHOR) found that 53% of participating HBO and university students reported (very) high stress in the previous four weeks, and 41% often felt performance pressure. Among students with at least some stress, study was the dominant source.

That load lands in shared kitchens and corridors. Temporary systems help more than vague “be considerate” appeals: a visible quiet calendar tied to exam dates, a short evening cooking window, and guest norms with a real opt-out.

New Domu Match editorial: Exam Week House Rules for Dutch Housemates.

## Instagram

Tentamenweek tip that is not another productivity hack:

Your house needs a temporary system, not a personality makeover.

National student monitoring (MMMS-2025) still finds heavy study stress and performance pressure among HBO and university students. Shared flats feel that first when someone cooks at 1am or starts a film night the evening before your exam.

Try three short agreements for the next 10–14 days:
1. Quiet hours on the calendar (with real dates)
2. Kitchen timing after a set hour
3. Guests announced, with an easy opt-out
