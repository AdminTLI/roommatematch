# Social posts — 2026-08-26

## LinkedIn

Students who leave home do not only change their address. They change who provides daily emotional support.

Hogeschool Inholland research among more than 6,500 students found that those living away from home report more loneliness, stress, and depressive symptoms than peers still living with parents. RIVM’s Gezondheidsmonitor Jongvolwassenen 2024 shows only about half of young adults rate their mental health as good. SCP’s 2025 youth work links parental emotional support to resilience.

The practical question for student houses is rarely “who cooks?” alone. It is also: how much listening do we expect from each other, when is a closed door respected, and when do we escalate beyond the flat?

Compatibility includes how housemates hold each other’s stress without becoming unpaid carers.

## Instagram

Moving out feels like freedom until a rough week hits and nobody knows the house rules for “I need support” vs “I need quiet.”

New Inholland findings: students living away from home report more loneliness and stress than those still at home. National RIVM data show many young adults already carry high study pressure.

So maybe the first house meeting should also ask:
• When is it ok to knock?
• Who has bandwidth to listen this week?
• Where do we point each other if it gets heavier than a flat can carry?

Support is a housemate skill. Boundaries are too.
