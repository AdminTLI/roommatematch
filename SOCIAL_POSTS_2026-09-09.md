## LinkedIn

Overnight guests expose a hidden split in Dutch student housing.

On one side: provider rules. SSH Short Stay terms ban overnight guests. Campus-style houses often set night limits and quiet hours in writing.

On the other: housemate consent. Many private student flats still run on “sometimes is fine,” until a partner’s toothbrush becomes a fifth resident and exam-week silence collapses.

Those are not the same conversation. One protects the building contract. The other protects dignity in a shared bathroom.

The useful question is procedural, not moral:
How many nights count as “sometimes”?
Who needs to say yes?
What changes in exam weeks?
Does the lease already forbid what the house chat has normalised?

Hospitality without capacity rules turns into occupation. Written guest nights and quiet hours are house infrastructure.

## Instagram

Your housemate said their partner “stays over sometimes.”

Cool. Define sometimes.

Is it two nights a week… or a toothbrush on the sink and a jacket that never leaves?

In a lot of Dutch student houses, the provider already has a rule (some ban overnights; others cap nights and set quiet hours). The house chat often invents a second, softer rule. Then everyone pretends those two rulebooks match.

They don’t.

Drop your house’s real overnight rule in the comments - written, spoken, or “we never talked about it.”
