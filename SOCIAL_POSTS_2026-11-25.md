## 1. LinkedIn

More than twenty Dutch mbo, hbo and university institutions told NOS Stories this month that they see more students asking for payment plans, money advice and financial help. Nibud's 2026 data points the same way: 54% of 18 to 30 year olds struggle to make ends meet, up from 40% in 2024.

The timing is practical, not just statistical. 2027 health insurance premiums are published, the mandatory eigen risico rises to EUR 400, zorgtoeslag rises to a maximum of EUR 140 a month, and the insurer switching window closes on 31 December. For international students the rules differ by residence and work status, which is exactly where a clear explainer from the institution helps.

Three things student welfare teams can do before the holidays: put the switching deadline and a zorgtoeslag income-update reminder in the December newsletter, make the studentendecaan booking link visible, and give students a script for asking for a betalingsregeling so asking feels routine.

Money stress grows in silence. Retention work includes making the first conversation easy.
