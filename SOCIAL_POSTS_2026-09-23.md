# Social posts - 2026-09-23

## LinkedIn

Dutch bachelor enrolments are not evenly spread across fields.

Rathenau data for 2022/2023 put **56%** of publicly funded bachelor places in the gamma sciences (social sciences, education, business, law). Bèta and technology took 20%, medical programmes 17%, and alfa fields 8%.

That concentration changes how study choice feels in practice:

- Popularity is density, not proof of fit.
- Master shares already rebalance toward technology (33%), while gamma still leads at 47%.
- National deadlines compress the decision: numerus fixus by 15 January, most bachelor registrations by 1 May.

For open-day season, the useful question is less “What is everyone studying?” and more “Which neighbouring programme inside this field matches the work I want to do - and which calendar gate applies?”

Sources: Rathenau Instituut; Rijksoverheid (studiekeuze en toelating); Studiekeuze123.
