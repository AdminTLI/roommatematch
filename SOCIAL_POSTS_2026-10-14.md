## 1. LinkedIn

Mid-October is not tentamenweek — and that is exactly why student houses struggle.

Tilburg University workload research shows end-of-period study load can spike toward ninety-four hours in a single week when deep learning is postponed until assessment crunch. The quieter operational problem sits in the weeks around those peaks: staggered midterms, shrinking daylight, and a recovery gap while new lectures have already started.

National student wellbeing monitoring still finds widespread emotional exhaustion and substantial hindrance from psychological complaints for a sizable minority. Shared housing cannot replace clinical care, but it can decide whether October adds friction or redistributes it.

Three mid-autumn systems that help:

1. A fortnight study map (focus windows, not a house lockdown)
2. A 48-hour soft landing after each person’s heaviest assessment
3. Low-demand indoor bonding (short shared dinners beat forced gezelligheid)

For housing and student-life teams: intro-week and exam-week guidance are not enough. The longest stretch of the semester needs a one-page mid-autumn addendum.
