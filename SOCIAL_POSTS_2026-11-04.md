## 1. LinkedIn

Two students, one Dutch house, two completely different weeks.

An HBO bachelor runs four years and 240 study points, with an average of about 25 contact hours a week and a compulsory internship. A WO bachelor runs three years and 180 points, with about 16 contact hours and more self-study. Both are full-time. Neither is "easier".

The 2026 Nationale Studenten Enquête (about 248,000 respondents) shows how much study pressure varies: 31% of students say it is too high, 34% of WO students versus 30% of HBO students, and 47% in education programmes versus 24% in economics.

For anyone thinking about student wellbeing and retention, the takeaway is practical. Pressure rarely shows from the next bedroom, but rhythm does. Internship exits, thesis seasons, and uneven graduation dates shape daily life long before they show up in a dropout statistic.

Sources: Landelijk Centrum Studiekeuze (NSE 2026), Universiteit Utrecht, Rijksoverheid, Nuffic.
