Article: [December Stay-Behind Loneliness for Students](/blog/december-empty-house-loneliness-netherlands)

## 1. LinkedIn

December student wellbeing is not only tentamenweek stress. It is what happens after the last exam, when suitcases leave and the corridor goes quiet.

Campus reporting (Delta / TU Delft) keeps spotting the same irony: advising internationals to go home thins the social net for those who cannot. MMMS-2025 still finds loneliness feelings widespread among HBO and WO students, with fatigue and loneliness more common among international respondents than in 2023.

Universities are already building counter-rituals - Leiden Winter Weeks (21 Dec 2026 to 2 Jan 2027), Winter University programmes, buddy matching. Student houses need a parallel habit: a leave/return roster, a stay-behind meal plan, and one check-in that is not a family-dinner photo dump.

Retention work in December is logistics plus belonging, not another wellbeing poster.

## 2. Instagram / WhatsApp

POV: your last exam is done… and so is half the huisgroepchat 🧳❄️

December has two lonely acts:
1. doors closed for studying
2. suitcases leaving for Christmas

If you’re staying (ticket too expensive / herexamen / work / just can’t go):
• map who stays which nights BEFORE the first train
• plan one cheap shared dinner every few days
• book one external anchor (Winter Weeks, ESN, buddy, walk)
• reopen the living room within 48h after your last exam

Leavers: drop your dates + leave milk/bin bags + send one non-flex check-in.

Who’s staying in your city this kerstvakantie - and did anyone actually ask?
