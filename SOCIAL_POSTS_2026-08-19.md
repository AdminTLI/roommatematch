# Social posts — 2026-08-19

## LinkedIn

Fewer Dutch students are searching for a room — not because supply recovered, but because years of scarcity taught cohorts to downgrade expectations early.

Kences’ 2025 Landelijke Monitor Studentenhuisvesting, reported by NOS, shows 44% of hbo and wo students live away from home, down from 52% eight years ago. Only 49% still say they want to live away, down from 59%. The gap between “want to” and “actually do” narrowed because fewer students still report the wish.

The headline shortage (~21,000 rooms) understates pressure when measured demand falls, graduates occupy student rooms, and mbo students who want to leave home are not yet in the national count. Supply in major cities fell by an estimated 13,500 rooms even as ~5,000 new units were built and private landlords sold thousands of student homes.

Worth reading if you work on student wellbeing, municipal housing policy, or university intake.
